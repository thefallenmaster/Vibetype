# 🎹 VibeType

**Type to the rhythm of your favorite videos!**

VibeType is a dynamic, beautifully designed typing test that lets you practice your typing speed and accuracy while watching a video in the background. It takes the traditional typing test and gives it a highly engaging, modern twist.

## 🚀 Interactive Features

- **Custom Videos**: Paste any MP4/WebM link or upload a local video to use as your visual rhythm.
- **Real-Time Stats**: Watch your WPM (Words Per Minute) and Accuracy update instantly as you hit each key.
- **Sleek Glassmorphism UI**: Built with a beautiful, modern aesthetic utilizing deep colors, smooth gradients, and frosted glass elements.
- **Auto-Focus & Shortcuts**: Start typing anywhere on the page to auto-focus the test! Use quick shortcuts like `Esc` to instantly restart or `Alt + C` to change the video.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Pick Your Vibe**: Open the site and paste a video URL or upload a file right from your device. 
2. **Start Typing**: Once the video loads and the text appears, just start typing. The hidden input will automatically catch your keystrokes.
3. **Track Your Flow**: Keep an eye on your WPM and Accuracy as they update live. Try to beat your high score!
4. **Reset**: Messed up? Just hit `Esc` to start over instantly.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **HTML5 & Vanilla CSS**: Utilizing modern features like CSS variables, flexbox, and keyframe animations.
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: Pure JS handles the typing logic, stat calculations, and state management without heavy frameworks.
- **Plyr.io**: A simple, accessible, and customizable HTML5 media player used for seamless video playback.

## 🏃‍♂️ Run it yourself!

VibeType runs entirely in the browser! No complex build tools, servers, or installations required.
Just double-click on `index.html` to open it in your favorite modern browser and start typing!
